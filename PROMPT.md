# SkillsMap AI — Application Specification & Prompt Reference

## Overview
SkillsMap is an AI-powered personalized engineering competency, roadmapping, adaptive timetable scheduling, and video lecture study platform.

## Key Features
1. **Adaptive Spaced Timetable Generator**:
   - Takes student name, target dates, preferred study hours, and personal condition text description.
   - Generates a day-by-day and hour-by-hour roadmap of what to study and when.
2. **Student Lectures Vault**:
   - Students upload video lectures (MP4/WebM) or embed YouTube streams.
   - Synchronized timestamp note-taking and AI key concepts extraction.
3. **Personalized AI Career Roadmaps**:
   - Step-by-step milestones with in-depth "Why Learn This?" explanations, industry demand rationale, and curated free resources/YouTube links.
4. **Course Documentation & ELI5 Guides**:
   - Deep multi-chapter engineering guides (Python, Web, DSA, System Design, Databases) with ELI5 metaphors and downloadable PDFs with clickable YouTube & free resource links.
5. **Role-Based Firebase Authentication**:
   - Google OAuth sign-in and protected Admin console for faculty.
6. **Collapsible Full-Screen UI**:
   - Full desktop sidebar collapse toggle and spacious full-screen layout.
